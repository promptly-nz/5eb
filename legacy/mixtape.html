Who Is 5EB

N17 born
N17 raised

5EB started out cutting bars over borrowed beats on a cracked copy of Cool Edit, passing CD-Rs round at the back of the bus. The tapes did the talking: no label, no budget, just a postcode and a pen.

Raised on pirate radio and the Sunday-night sets on the estate, he writes like a diary and records like it's the last session before the landlord changes the locks. Raw, quick, honest — in the tradition of the ones who made it sound like home.

"If they can't hear the road in it, I ain't done it right."
